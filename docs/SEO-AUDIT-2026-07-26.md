### Finding T1 — canonical/sitemap point at the wrong live domain (Medium, preview-only)
`SITE.url` is hardcoded to `https://truthcaregroup.co.uk` ([site.ts](../site/src/lib/site.ts)) and used verbatim for `<link rel="canonical">` and every sitemap `<loc>` entry — including on the **currently live, public, crawlable** GitHub Pages preview. Right now:
- The preview at `mcfearless75.github.io/truthcare` is fully allowed in robots.txt with no noindex, but every page tells crawlers "the canonical version of me lives at truthcaregroup.co.uk" — a domain not yet resolving to this content.
- This is the correct end-state for after DNS cutover, but until then it's a bit of a coin-flip whether Google indexes the GH Pages URL anyway (cross-domain canonical claims aren't always honoured, especially before the target domain exists).

**Fix:** add a noindex meta tag (or `X-Robots-Tag` header) specifically on the GitHub Pages preview build via the `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH` env check already used for the Pages build, so the preview can be shared/reviewed without any indexing ambiguity. Remove it (or make it conditional) once DNS cuts over and this same build path stops being used for the public preview.

### Finding T2 — Open Graph image URL is broken on the current preview build (Low, self-resolves at cutover)
`layout.tsx` sets `metadataBase: new URL(SITE.url)` (`https://truthcaregroup.co.uk`) and builds the OG image path via `withBasePath("/images/.../1200.jpg")`, which prepends the GH Pages basePath (`/truthcare`). Combined, the resulting `og:image` on the live preview is:

```
https://truthcaregroup.co.uk/truthcare/images/beaconsfield-house-exterior-front/1200.jpg
```

That URL doesn't resolve on either domain right now — `truthcaregroup.co.uk` isn't live yet, and even once it is, production builds won't have the `/truthcare` prefix. **Right now, sharing the GH Pages preview link in Slack/WhatsApp/LinkedIn/Twitter shows a broken image card.** Confirmed via direct fetch of the served HTML.

**Fix:** only relevant if the preview link gets shared externally for review — either set `metadataBase` conditionally to the GH Pages origin when `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH` is set, or accept it as a known preview-only cosmetic issue since production (no basePath) will resolve correctly automatically.

## 3. Content quality / E-E-A-T

### Content Quality Score: 58/100

| Factor | Score /25 | Notes |

### Findings

**Critical**
- No CQC **rating** (e.g. "Good"/"Outstanding") or provider ID shown anywhere tied to Beaconsfield House itself — only a generic "registered with the CQC" line, plus a vague rating mention buried in a team bio that isn't tied to this location. CQC rating is one of the highest-trust signals in UK care-sector content and it's currently invisible.
- The ~15 `legal.ts` items already awaiting your sign-off (company number, ICO registration, DPO status) carry direct Trustworthiness weight — same known open item, not a new one.

**High**
- `home.ts` (~213 words) and `services.ts` (~120 words) fall well short of typical homepage (500w) / service-page (800w) topical-coverage floors. `services.ts` in particular is an intro paragraph + bullet list with no admission process, funding/commissioner pathway, staffing ratios, or outcomes detail — exactly what a case manager or commissioner needs to see on a decision-driving page.
- "Delivering evidence-based intervention with **measurable outcomes**" (homepage) is asserted with zero supporting data, case study, or metric anywhere on the site.

**Medium**
- Repetitive phrasing across pages ("person-centred rehabilitation," "goal focused community rehabilitation") — a recognised low-quality-AI-content marker per the Sept-2025 Google Quality Rater Guidelines update, even though the copy is plausibly human-written.
- No author/reviewer attribution on clinical-adjacent FAQ content (capacity, legal frameworks) on /who-we-support.

**Low / strength**
- `team.ts` bios are the single best E-E-A-T asset on the site (specific registrations, named institutions — Frenchay, Southmead, BNSSG) but are only linked from `/our-team`.

### Recommendations
1. Surface the real CQC rating + provider ID on `/contact-us` and the homepage.
2. Expand `services.ts` and `home.ts` toward the topical-coverage floors: admission pathway, funding routes, staffing ratios, one anonymised outcome vignette.
3. Add a named-clinician review credit to clinical-adjacent FAQ content.
4. Resolve the outstanding `legal.ts` sign-off items — this is already tracked, just note it also has an SEO/trust dimension.
5. Cross-link `/our-team` bios from the service and support pages so the Expertise signal isn't siloed on one page.
