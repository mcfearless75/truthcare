# Truth Care Group — Full SEO Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-13 · **Domain audited:** https://truthcaregroup.co.uk (live production, post-DNS-cutover)
**Method:** Direct HTTP inspection of every live page, Google SERP verification (not estimated), plus competitor research carried forward from the 2026-07-26 audit (re-validated where it affects conclusions below).

---

## Executive Summary

The site's biggest strength is technical cleanliness: every page is fully indexable, self-canonical, fast, and already ranking #1 for the brand's own name with 5+ pages re-indexed within a day of cutover. The three highest-impact priorities are the same three flagged three weeks ago and still open: **no CQC rating displayed anywhere**, **no outcomes/case-study evidence**, and **no funding/commissioning pathway page** — all three are what case managers and commissioners (the actual buyers in this sector) look for first, and every credible competitor has at least one of them. One content gap has closed since the last audit: **the downloadable brochure is now live**, matching a specific competitor advantage flagged before. Overall assessment: **strong technical foundation, content/trust layer needs work** — this is not a "rebuild the site" problem, it's a "write four more pieces of content" problem.
